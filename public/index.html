Goal: forecast daily attendance a week ahead — far enough out to set staff schedules and buy ads. The week-ahead model only sees data available 7+ days before each day; the day-ahead model (shown as a reference) has fresh data and marks the ceiling. Labor budget — what forecast misses cost

You schedule staff to the forecast. A day that runs quieter than planned wastes paid hours; a day that runs busier than planned leaves shifts short. Two assumptions convert guest misses into labor — set them to your operation and the whole panel rescales.

Marketing — could soft days be seen a week out?

A soft day is a bottom-quartile attendance day — worth stimulating with a promotion or ad buy, but only if it's flagged early enough to act. Chips show every soft-day call, in order.
