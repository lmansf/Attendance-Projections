Attendance Forecast — Staffing & Ad-Spend

Goal: forecast daily attendance at the horizons decisions are made: month-ahead for the labor budget and roster lock (that model sees only data knowable 30+ days out — no weather), and week-ahead for schedule adjustments and ad buys (data knowable 7+ days out). The day-ahead model is the reference ceiling with fresh data. Everything below measures those forecasts or prices their misses.

Labor budget — misses priced at the month-ahead lock

Labor budgets and rosters commit about a month out, so this panel prices the month-ahead model's misses. A day that runs quieter than planned wastes paid hours; a day that runs busier than planned leaves shifts short. Two assumptions convert guest misses into labor — set them to your operation and the whole panel rescales.

Marketing — could soft days be seen a week out?

A soft day is a bottom-quartile attendance day — worth stimulating with a promotion or ad buy, but only if it's flagged early enough to act. Chips show every soft-day call, in order.
