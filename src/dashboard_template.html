Attendance Forecast — Staffing & Ad-Spend

Goal: predict daily attendance far enough ahead to set labor schedules and time ad spend. The week-ahead model uses only information available 7+ days out (when schedules lock and media is bought); day-ahead is the fine-tuning ceiling. Every panel below measures that ability or explains its failures — nothing else.
